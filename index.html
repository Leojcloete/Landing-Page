About the Odyssey

Origin Path: Odyssey™ is an interactive journey of self-discovery built around familiar gaming scenarios rather than rigid tests or career formulas. By exploring how you naturally approach challenges, tactical decisions, and squad missions, this experience maps your strengths across seven core Tenno Archetypes, such as the Master Builder, Tactical Analyst, Clan Commander, and more.

This is not a questionnaire designed to label you or dictate your future. It is a starting transmission to help you recognize the unique contributions you already bring to every team, mission, and real-world environment you encounter.

Who Is This For?

Young Gamers & Explorers (Ages 15–25)

As well as anyone seeking to understand their natural strengths, problem-solving styles, and real-world potential through a familiar gaming lens.

Supporters (Parents, Mentors, Teachers & Guides)

Anyone looking for practical insights, strength-based conversation starters, and actionable ways to encourage a young person's journey and future life adventures.
